APPROACH

Biography

Tlielaxu began practicing in 2003, working with Olympic hopefuls and the UC Berkeley swim and water polo teams. His early exposure to sports therapy and his father’s influence in chiropractic still inform how each session is built.


Through the lens of Biomechanical Sports Therapy, each session draws from Active Release Techniques, Somatics, Deep Tissue, Thai, Tui Na, Ashiatsu, Esalen, and LaStone Therapy, then adapts in real time to the needs of the individual athlete.
